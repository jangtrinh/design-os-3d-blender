# Project context

## Durable lesson

Every acceptance claim belongs to a specific artifact revision and a check that
can reject a wrong result. Keep requirements independent of measurements, retain
the scope and uncertainty of each predicate, and invalidate only the affected
evidence after a change. A valid journal, render, mesh, logical circuit or specimen
form cannot substitute for a different kind of evidence.

Operational entry: `.agents/skills/blender-agent-core/SKILL.md`.
Worked retrospective: `docs/ck-001-session-retrospective.md`.

Terminology: use **executed**, **digital prototype**, **engineering candidate**,
**pilot evidence** and **manufacture** for their separate scopes. Avoid using
**production-ready**, **functional**, **continuous collision-free** or **certified**
as synonyms for a passing geometry or visual check.
