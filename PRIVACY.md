# Privacy Policy (`design-os-3d-blender`)

`design-os-3d-blender` (`DESIGN:OS 3D Blender Agent`) is a local-first, source-available engineering tool and AI agent skill in the **DESIGN:OS** ecosystem.

## Zero Telemetry Guarantee

- **No network calls by the skill or CLI**: `design-os-3d-blender` does not phone home, collect analytics, or transmit source code, prompts, or generated artifacts to any external server.
- **No third-party tracking**: There are no embedded telemetry beacons, crash reporters, or usage trackers in `skills/`, `scripts/`, or plugin manifests.
- **100% local execution**: All file reads, validations, and builds happen inside your local workspace and under your active AI coding agent's permission model.
- **Auditable source**: Every script and prompt in this repository is plain-text Markdown, Python, or JavaScript/Swift under the [PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

## Contact

For privacy or security questions, see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) or open an issue on [`jangtrinh/design-os-3d-blender`](https://github.com/jangtrinh/design-os-3d-blender).
