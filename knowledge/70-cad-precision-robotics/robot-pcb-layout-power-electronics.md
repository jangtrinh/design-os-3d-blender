# Robot PCB Layout & Power Electronics: Gate Loops, Kelvin Sensing & Thermal Vias

> Production-grade PCB design rules for high-density BLDC motor controllers: 4-to-6 layer stackups, minimizing parasitic gate inductance ($<5\,\text{nH}$), Kelvin current-shunt routing, solid ground-plane integrity, and thermal via arrays.

---

## Purpose
Collects layout rules for a high-current BLDC motor-driver board: small gate loops, Kelvin shunt sensing, an unbroken ground plane and thermal-via arrays under power FETs. Design-reference note, not certification: threshold numbers are mostly unverified heuristics; follow the FET, driver and fab datasheets and run a real EMC and thermal test.

## When to Use / When NOT
- ALLOWED: laying out or reviewing a 24-60 V multi-amp half-bridge board: gate loop, shunt routing, via array, stackup
- ALLOWED: estimating via-array thermal resistance from geometry before a thermal test
- NOT ALLOWED: DC-bus pre-charge, brake chopper and TVS sizing — use `power-distribution-bms-protection` instead
- NOT ALLOWED: FOC control loops and motor models — use `actuators-motors-foc-control` instead
- NOT ALLOWED: EMC test planning and certification — use `emc-emi-environmental-qualification` instead

## Core Knowledge Content

### 1. Physical Principles & Governing Equations

#### A. Gate Loop Parasitic Inductance & Voltage Overshoot
In high-current MOSFET H-bridges, rapid switching ($di/dt > 500\,\text{A}/\mu\text{s}$) across parasitic gate loop inductance ($L_{gate}$) induces destructive ringing and false Miller turn-on: <!-- ease:source S1 anchor="Miller effect: C_GD,eqv = (1 + gfs x RL) x C_GD (Eq. 4); capacitive-divider form here is UNVERIFIED" -->

```math
V_{ringing} = L_{gate} \frac{di_{gate}}{dt}, \quad V_{Miller} = \frac{C_{gd}}{C_{gd} + C_{gs}} \cdot \frac{dV_{DS}}{dt}
```

- **Target Threshold:** Parasitic loop inductance must remain strictly below **$5.0\,\text{nH}$**. <!-- ease:source UNVERIFIED reason="5 nH gate-loop target not stated in S1; S1 treats parasitic inductances qualitatively" -->
- **Layout Rule:** Gate resistor must sit $<5.0\,\text{mm}$ from the MOSFET gate pin. Return trace must route directly on the layer immediately beneath the gate trace to minimize magnetic flux area. <!-- ease:source UNVERIFIED reason="5 mm distance is a heuristic" -->

#### B. Kelvin Current Sensing
A current sense shunt resistor ($R_{shunt} \approx 1 - 5\,\text{m}\Omega$) measuring 20–50A motor currents will experience huge measurement errors if PCB trace resistance ($R_{trace} \approx 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega$) is included in the path: <!-- ease:source UNVERIFIED reason="trace-resistance error is derived arithmetic from the quoted values" -->

```math
V_{measured} = I_{phase} \cdot \left( R_{shunt} + R_{trace} \right) \quad (\text{error} = R_{trace}/R_{shunt}\text{: 10\% to 50\% for the quoted values})
```

- **Kelvin (4-Wire) Discipline:** Sense traces must tap directly from the **inside inner pads** of the shunt component footprint and route as a tightly coupled differential pair into the current-sense amplifier (e.g. INA240 / DRV8301) with zero return ground sharing. <!-- ease:source UNVERIFIED reason="Kelvin tapping is standard practice; no source captured; INA240/DRV8301 datasheets not captured" -->

#### C. Thermal Via Heat Conduction (Fourier's Law)
Thermal energy generated by MOSFET package exposed pads ($P_{loss} = I_{rms}^2 R_{DS(on)}$) conducts through vertical copper via barrels: <!-- ease:source UNVERIFIED reason="formula is Fourier conduction through a copper annulus, derived in-module" -->

```math
R_{th,via} = \frac{h_{board}}{k_{Cu} \cdot \pi \left( r_{outer}^2 - r_{inner}^2 \right)}
```

For a typical $1.6\,\text{mm}$ board, a single via with $0.3\,\text{mm}$ hole and $25\,\mu\text{m}$ copper plating has $R_{th} \approx 160\,\text{K/W}$. An array of 16 stitched vias reduces thermal resistance to about $10\,\text{K/W}$ (derived 2026-10-13: 162.8 / 16 = 10.2 K/W with $k_{Cu}=385\,\text{W/(m K)}$, ideal parallel conduction, no spreading; the former "<10" was slightly optimistic). <!-- ease:source UNVERIFIED reason="derived: 1.6e-3/(385 x pi x (0.175^2-0.15^2) mm^2) = 162.8 K/W; copper conductivity assumed" -->

### 3. High-Density Layer Stackup Architecture

```text
Layer 1 (Top Signal & Power):
  ├── Gate Drivers (DRV830x / TMC6300)
  ├── High-Side & Low-Side MOSFETs (Direct FET / DFN 5x6)
  └── Shunt Resistors (Kelvin Tapped)
        │ (16-Via Thermal Array, 0.3mm drill, no thermal relief)
Layer 2 (Solid Ground Plane):
  └── 100% Continuous Unbroken Copper GND (Lowest return path inductance)
Layer 3 (DC Bus Power Plane - 48V):
  └── Bulk decoupling capacitors (X7R ceramic array + Low-ESR Polymer)
Layer 4 (Bottom Signal & Logic):
  └── MCU (STM32H7 / ESP32-S3), CAN-FD transceiver, encoder interfaces
```

- **Unbroken Ground Plane Law:** Never route signal traces across Layer 2. Any split or slot in the ground plane forces high-frequency return currents to loop around the void, turning the PCB into an efficient dipole antenna and failing EMC certification. <!-- ease:source UNVERIFIED reason="layout practice; no source captured" -->
- **Thermal Pad Connection:** Thermal pads of power MOSFETs must solder directly into solid copper flood with **zero thermal reliefs** (thermal reliefs are for hand soldering; power boards require automated reflow). <!-- ease:source UNVERIFIED reason="zero-relief pads are a manufacturer-dependent choice; check the package land-pattern note" -->

### 4. Failure Modes & Mitigation Matrix (DFMEA)

| Failure Mode | Root Cause | Failure Effect | Engineering Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| MOSFET Cross-Conduction (Shoot-Through) | High $dV/dt$ induces Miller turn-on in low-side FET | Instantaneous short circuit across DC bus, exploded silicon | Gate pull-down resistor ($10\,\text{k}\Omega$) + active Miller clamp circuit / bipolar gate drive ($-5\text{V} / +10\text{V}$). | <!-- ease:source UNVERIFIED reason="10 kOhm pull-down, Miller clamp and -5/+10 V gate drive are design heuristics" -->
| Current Sense Offset Drift | Ground return current flowing through analog sense trace | Torque ripple, FOC current loop instability | Dedicated analog ground plane island ($AGND$) tied to power ground at a single star point. |
| Solder Voiding & Starvation | Solder wicking into open thermal via barrels | Overheating MOSFET dies, premature thermal shutdown | Via tenting or epoxy via plugging (VIPPO: Via-In-Pad Plated Over). | <!-- ease:source UNVERIFIED reason="VIPPO advice not sourced here" -->
| Trace Fuse Burning | High transient motor stall current exceeding PCB trace width | Delaminated PCB copper, open circuit | Use Saturn PCB Toolkit to size trace widths for $<15^\circ\text{C}$ temperature rise under continuous RMS load. | <!-- ease:source UNVERIFIED reason="Saturn PCB Toolkit not captured" -->

### 5. Production Verification Gate

#### Level 1: DFM & Gerber Electrical Rule Checks
- [ ] IPC-2221B electrical clearance audited across all 48V nets (code minimum 0.6 mm for B2 per S3; 1.5 mm kept as design margin; creepage is a separate safety-standard concept).
- [ ] Via-in-pad specifications documented on fabrication drawing (type VII filled and capped).

#### Level 2: Low-Voltage Signal & Gate Integrity
- [ ] Oscilloscope check at MOSFET gate with active differential probe: Gate ringing peak $\le 10\%$ of drive voltage; zero negative spikes exceeding $-0.3\,\text{V}$. <!-- ease:source UNVERIFIED reason="10 percent ringing and -0.3 V limits are project heuristics" -->
- [ ] Differential Kelvin shunt traces verified with micro-ohmmeter: resistance matching within $1\%$.

#### Level 3: Full Load Thermal Soak Test
- [ ] Board operated at continuous full phase current ($30\,\text{A}$) for 60 minutes: Thermal camera confirms MOSFET junction-to-ambient temperature rise $\Delta T \le 45\,\text{K}$. <!-- ease:source UNVERIFIED reason="45 K rise limit is a project heuristic" -->

#### Level 4: Manufacturing Release File Pack
- [ ] Complete fabrication and assembly package: ODB++ / Gerber RS-274X, IPC-D-356 netlist, pick-and-place centroid file, and paste mask stencil drawing ($1:1$ with $15\%$ cross-hatched reduction on thermal pads). <!-- ease:source UNVERIFIED reason="release-pack contents are a checklist, not a standard clause" -->

## Failure Modes (Mandatory)
- **Shoot-through or ringing at switching edges** — cause: large gate loop inductance and Miller turn-on of the off-state FET — fix: short gate loop, gate resistor close to the pin, pull-down or Miller clamp (concept: S1)
- **Current reading is high and offset by load** — cause: shunt sensed through shared trace resistance — fix: Kelvin taps from the inner pads; derived error is R_trace/R_shunt (10 to 50 percent for the quoted values)
- **Overheating FET despite a via array** — cause: vias wicked open or too few for the package — fix: tent/plug vias; TI guidance is 5-9 vias for a small die at 0.33 mm drill (S2); the module's 16-via claim is derived, not tested
- **Clearance audit disagrees with the layout rule** — cause: this note used 1.5 mm and 1.25 mm as IPC-2221B 48 V values; the table value is 0.6 mm for uncoated external conductors (S3) — fix: label 1.5 mm as design margin
- **Sense amplifier offset drifts** — cause: power ground current in the analog return — fix: single-point analog ground tie
